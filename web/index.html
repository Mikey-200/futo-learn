Upload Any Course Material

Drop in any PDF or DOCX lecture note. The app extracts the text automatically — even from scanned documents using AI-powered OCR.

AI-Powered Personalised Lessons

Tell the AI what you already know and what you want to learn — it generates a structured lesson tailored specifically to your knowledge level.

Smart Quiz System

Test yourself with 7 AI-generated multiple-choice questions based on your actual lesson. Get instant feedback and explanations on every answer.

Multiple Lesson Versions

Not happy with the lesson? Regenerate with a specific focus — "explain step by step" or "use more examples" — and keep all versions to compare.

Progress Dashboard

Track your study stats at a glance — materials uploaded, lessons generated, average quiz score, and your recent activity all in one home screen.

Light & Dark Mode

Switch between a clean light mode for daytime study and a gorgeous dark mode for late-night cramming sessions. Your preference is saved automatically.
